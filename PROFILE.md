# AI Notarization & Proof

Cryptographic receipts for AI inference by ForgeMesh Labs — signed Ed25519 attestations, sha256 content hashes, and Merkle-chain anchors, paid per call in USDC via x402. Notarize and verify AI model outputs to prove a specific model produced a specific output at a specific time. Verification and read endpoints are free; notarization is pay-per-call micropayments with no accounts or API keys.
